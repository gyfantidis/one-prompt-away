## My Excel Confession

I've been avoiding Excel for years. Every time someone sent me a spreadsheet, I'd panic. VLOOKUP? SUMIF? Those words meant nothing to me.

Then I discovered something that changed everything. I can describe what I want in plain English, and AI writes the formula for me. No tutorials. No courses. Just one prompt.

## How This Actually Works

You open ChatGPT (or any free AI chat tool like Claude or Google Gemini). You describe your problem like you're talking to a friend. The AI gives you the exact formula to copy and paste.

That's it. Really.

## The Prompt Template That Works Every Time

Here's the structure I use:

prompt
I have an Excel spreadsheet with [describe your columns].
I need a formula that [what you want to calculate].
The data starts in row 2. Row 1 has headers.
Give me the formula and explain what each part does in simple terms.


Let me show you real examples.

## Example 1: Calculate Total Sales

I have sales data. Column A has product names. Column B has quantities. Column C has prices. I need the total revenue.

prompt
I have an Excel spreadsheet with:
- Column A: Product names
- Column B: Quantities sold
- Column C: Price per item

I need a formula that calculates total revenue (quantity × price) for each row.
Data starts in row 2.
Give me the formula and explain it simply.


The AI responds with: `=B2*C2`

And explains: "This multiplies the quantity in B2 by the price in C2. Drag it down to apply to all rows."

Simple. Done.

## Example 2: Find Names That Match a Condition

This is where it gets powerful. I needed to sum only sales from a specific salesperson.

prompt
I have a spreadsheet with:
- Column A: Salesperson name
- Column B: Sale amount

I need a formula that adds up all sales ONLY for "Maria".
Data is in rows 2 to 100.
Explain what each part means.


The AI gives me: `=SUMIF(A2:A100,"Maria",B2:B100)`

And breaks it down:
- SUMIF means "sum if a condition is true"
- A2:A100 is where to look for the name
- "Maria" is what to look for
- B2:B100 is what to add up when found

I would have never figured this out on my own. Now I understand it.

## Example 3: Look Up Information From Another Sheet

This one used to terrify me. I have a product code and need to find its price from a separate list.

prompt
I have two sheets in Excel.

Sheet1 has:
- Column A: Product codes I'm working with

Sheet2 has:
- Column A: All product codes
- Column B: Prices for each code

I need a formula in Sheet1 that looks up each product code and brings back its price from Sheet2.
Explain VLOOKUP to me like I'm 10 years old.


The AI gives me the formula AND explains it with an analogy about looking up a phone number in a contacts list. Finally, VLOOKUP makes sense.

## Pro Tips I Learned Along the Way

**Always mention your row numbers.** Tell the AI where your data starts and ends. "Data is in rows 2 to 50" prevents errors.

**Ask for explanations.** Don't just copy the formula. Ask the AI to explain each part. You'll actually learn something.

**Test with simple data first.** Before applying a formula to 10,000 rows, test it on 5 rows. Make sure it works.

**When something breaks, describe the error.** If Excel shows #REF! or #VALUE!, paste that error into the AI chat. Ask what went wrong. It usually knows.

## What About Google Sheets?

Same prompts work. Just mention "Google Sheets" instead of "Excel". Most formulas are identical. When they're different, the AI will give you the right version.
