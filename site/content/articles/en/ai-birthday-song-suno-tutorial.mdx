## What is Suno?

Suno is an AI tool that creates complete songs — vocals, instruments, everything — from a text description. You type what you want, and it generates a full track.

No music skills needed. No software to install. You just write and it sings.

The free tier gives you 50 credits per day. Each song costs about 5 credits, so you can make around 10 songs daily without paying anything.

## Step 1: Go to Suno

Open your browser and visit suno.com. Sign up with your Google account or email. Takes 30 seconds.

Once you're in, you'll see a simple text box. That's where the magic happens.

## Step 2: Write Your Prompt

This is the key part. Your prompt tells Suno what kind of song to make.

Here's the exact prompt I used for George:

prompt
Upbeat acoustic birthday song for my friend George who just turned 40. He loves basketball, hates mornings, and his kids call him "papa bear." Include his name in the chorus. Make it fun and heartfelt, not cheesy. Verse about getting older but still being young at heart.


The more specific details you add, the more personal the song feels.

## What to Include in Your Prompt

- The person's name (it will actually sing their name)
- Their age or milestone
- Hobbies or interests
- Inside jokes or nicknames
- The vibe you want (fun, emotional, silly, epic)
- Music style (acoustic, pop, rock, jazz — whatever fits them)

## Step 3: Pick a Style

After you write your prompt, Suno asks for a style. You can type things like:

- "acoustic pop"
- "upbeat indie folk"
- "80s rock ballad"
- "jazz lounge"

Think about what music your friend actually likes. For George, I picked "acoustic pop with warm vocals" because he's into singer-songwriter stuff.

## Step 4: Generate and Listen

Hit the Create button. Suno generates two versions of your song — usually about 1-2 minutes each.

Listen to both. One might nail the vibe better than the other.

Don't like either? Just hit create again. You have plenty of free credits.

## Step 5: Download and Share

Once you find the version you love, click the download button. You'll get an MP3 file.

From there, you can:

- Send it via WhatsApp or email
- Play it at the party from your phone
- Add it to a video montage
- Make it their ringtone (extra points)

## Tips for Better Results

I made about five birthday songs this year for different friends. Here's what I learned:

**Be specific about names.** If it's an unusual name, spell it phonetically in the prompt. For "Giorgos" I wrote "George" to help the AI pronounce it right.

**Mention the chorus.** Saying "include their name in the chorus" makes it feel more like a real birthday song.

**Keep it short.** The AI does better with focused prompts. Don't write a novel — three to five sentences work perfectly.

**Try different styles.** Same prompt with "country" versus "pop" gives completely different results. Experiment.
