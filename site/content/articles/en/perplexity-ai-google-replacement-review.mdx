## The Experiment

Three weeks ago, I did something dramatic. I removed Google as my default search engine and replaced it with Perplexity AI. No backup plan, no "just in case" bookmarks.

Why? Because I was tired of scrolling past ads, SEO-stuffed articles, and Reddit threads from 2019 just to find a simple answer.

Here's what actually happened.

## What Perplexity Does Differently

Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine. You ask a question, and instead of giving you 10 blue links, it gives you a direct answer with sources cited inline.

Think of it as ChatGPT meets Google, but with real-time web access and actual citations you can verify.

The free tier gives you 5 "Pro" searches per day (using GPT-4 or Claude) plus unlimited basic searches. I've been using the Pro plan at $20/month.

## Where Perplexity Crushed Google

### Research Questions

This is where Perplexity shines. Instead of opening 7 tabs and synthesizing information yourself, you get a summary with sources.

prompt
What are the main differences between RAG and fine-tuning for LLMs? Give me practical examples of when to use each.


Google would give me documentation pages, Medium articles, and maybe a relevant Reddit thread. Perplexity gave me a structured comparison with examples, citing actual papers and documentation.

Time saved: at least 15 minutes per complex query.

### Current Events

Perplexity's real-time search actually works. I asked about a tech announcement that happened 2 hours earlier, and it found the information from multiple sources.

Google's results were still showing articles from the previous week.

### "How To" With Context

prompt
How do I set up a redirect in Next.js 14 App Router? I'm using the new app directory structure.


Perplexity understood the context and gave me the current syntax. Google's top results were still showing Pages Router examples from Next.js 12.

## Where I Went Crawling Back to Google

### Local Searches

"Coffee shops near me" — Perplexity tries, but it's not great. No maps integration, no reviews, no opening hours displayed nicely. I had to open Google Maps anyway.

### Shopping

Looking for a specific product? Google's shopping results, price comparisons, and merchant reviews are still unmatched. Perplexity gave me general recommendations but couldn't help me find the best deal on a specific item.

### Quick Facts

"Weather tomorrow" or "USD to EUR" — Google's instant answers in the search bar are faster. Perplexity works, but it's overkill for these queries.

## The Unexpected Benefits

### I Ask Better Questions Now

Because Perplexity rewards specificity, I've trained myself to ask clearer questions. This carries over to how I prompt other AI tools too.

### Fewer Rabbit Holes

Without 10 tabs begging to be opened, I actually finish what I'm researching. Get answer, verify source, move on.

### Built-in Fact Checking

Every claim has a citation number. Click it, verify it. This has saved me from sharing wrong information at least twice.

## My Current Setup

After 30 days, here's what I landed on:

- **Default search engine**: Perplexity (for research, explanations, current events)
- **Bookmarked**: Google Maps (local search), Google Shopping (purchases)
- **Keyboard shortcut**: I set up a quick shortcut to Google for the rare times I need it

I'm using Perplexity for maybe 70% of my searches now.

## The Honest Verdict

Perplexity isn't a complete Google replacement. Not yet.

But for knowledge work — research, learning, staying current — it's genuinely better. The time I save not parsing through SEO garbage is worth the $20/month alone.

If you're a student, researcher, marketer, or anyone who needs to learn things fast, try it for a week. The free tier is enough to test whether it fits your workflow.

## Your Action Step

Before you go:

1. Go to perplexity.ai
2. Ask it one question you'd normally Google
3. Compare the experience

That's it. You'll know in 60 seconds if it's for you.

I'm not saying delete Google tomorrow. But maybe don't let it be your only option anymore.
